My Background

I’m currently working as an Implementation Team Lead at Smokeball, where I manage the onboarding experience for law firms transitioning onto our platform. My role focuses on guiding clients through complex system changes, coordinating across internal teams, and making sure each implementation is structured, efficient and sets the firm up for long-term success.

Through this work, I’ve developed a strong interest in how technology supports real-world business operations. I enjoy breaking down complex processes, improving workflows, and finding practical ways to use software to solve problems.

Alongside my professional experience, I’m studying Information Technology to deepen my technical understanding. This has given me a solid foundation in web development, systems thinking and how digital tools are designed and built. My goal is to continue building both my technical and strategic skills, combining practical experience with formal study to create solutions that are not only functional, but genuinely useful for the people using them.
